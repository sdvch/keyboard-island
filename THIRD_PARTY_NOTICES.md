## Excluded recordings

The earlier hosted demo used prerecorded Microsoft Xiaoyi neural speech generated through `edge-tts`/Edge Read Aloud. No recording or speech-generation client is distributed in this source release. We have not established a redistribution grant for that output; do not assume that a TTS client's source license licenses service output. The 147 first-party scripts remain available for replacement recording.

This is an asset-inclusion decision for this repository, not a claim about every possible use of a speech service. A future voice pack must document its producer, provenance, permitted use and redistribution terms before inclusion. The hosted demo's recordings are not covered by this repository's MIT license.
